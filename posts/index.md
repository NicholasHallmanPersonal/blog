### Why a blog?

I'm a senior software developer and I've always had a hard time with writing. I didn't care much for it in high school and as a result I never got good high grades on any essays or other school reports. I have however, always enjoyed public speaking. I can be fairly convincing in person and I've always enjoyed performing. English presentation were always a bump to my grades and I never got below a 90%. Of course, this only gets you so far. I can't be everywhere I want to share an idea but I still need those ideas to travel. Practice makes perfect so that's what all this is, a space to practice. Working on my writing will make me a better presenter as well, give my stories better structure, more impactful conclusion, and allow me to organize my thoughts better. That is one thing I've noticied when I use AI and something I'd noticed before when thinking up the best google search query. Often thinking about how to explain a problem to someone else can be all the context you need to overcome a mental block. Hopefully writing more will lead to more of those kinds of insights for me. So I have a lot of reasons to learn to write better and it's going to be very rough to start. This into is likely proof of that. A stream of consiousness mess to anchor my progress. 

Well, that's that. I'm hoping to have a few interesting this to share here. Some of this will just be practice and pretty boring and I'll likely need some kind of system to hide those posts, maybe a private folder... Again, thinking in real time here.

Well that's enough rambling. Till next time.
